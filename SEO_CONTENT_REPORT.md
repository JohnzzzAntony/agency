The production domain has not been purchased. No domain or canonical URLs were invented. The old sitemap pointed to another agency and has been replaced with an explicitly pending empty sitemap; do not submit it. After the domain is chosen, generate absolute sitemap URLs, canonical URLs and og:url values, then verify the deployed site in Google Search Console. Use the actual hosting behavior for redirects and trailing-slash conventions.

Existing URLs intentionally remain unchanged, including legacy paths such as services/webflow for Shopify and case-studies/kodezi for Karji. Their labels and contents now match DuooNex.
