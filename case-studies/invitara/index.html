Invitara: interactive invitation platform

Interactive invitation platform

Overview

Invitara is an invitation platform with five distinct experiences for weddings, galas, birthdays and family celebrations. Customers choose a design, personalise content and share a published guest link. The product combines animated storytelling with an editor, RSVP collection and an account dashboard.

The application uses Node.js, Express, SQLite and a locally bundled GSAP frontend. Vellum, Nocturne, Confetti Club, Little Bloom and Atlas each have their own composition. The original screenshots show the storefront, invitation themes and mobile editor; the film belongs to the Nocturne experience.

Journey

How Invitara works

A visitor previews a theme and creates a personalised draft before purchase. Verified checkout unlocks the purchased experience. The owner can publish a guest link, collect replies and review responses, with editing access governed by the selected event date and timezone.

  • Five independent invitation compositions
  • Editable content, images and section order
  • Guest replies and CSV export
  • Account-based saves and publishing
Engineering

Design and development focus

The server checks purchase status and expiry for save and publish actions. Stripe redirects alone do not unlock a purchase: the application verifies signed webhook events. Password hashing, account sessions and private owner access protect the editing and RSVP workflows.

  • Guest replies and CSV export
  • Account-based saves and publishing
  • Stripe webhook purchase verification
  • Timezone-aware event expiry
Process

How we delivered Invitara

The build followed four connected stages, from structuring the core workflow to reviewing it on real devices.

01

Explore

Preview five themes with distinct typography, motion and opening interactions.

02

Connect

Personalise text, photos, colours and section order in the editor.

03

Build

Publish after verified purchase and share the invitation with guests.

04

Review

Manage guest replies and retain the invitation after the event ends.

Scope

Implementation and scope

Drafts and previews work without payment credentials. Live checkout requires configured Stripe keys and webhook delivery. After the event, the editor becomes read-only and replies close while the published invitation remains available as a keepsake.

Invitara connects expressive web design with a practical content and purchase workflow. A similar event product should define who can edit, how guests respond, when access expires and what remains available after the event.
