/ Project overview

Karji Perfumes brings a broad luxury retail catalogue into one storefront. The website combines fragrance brands, watches and accessories with product discovery and shopping actions. The project scope centres on catalogue structure, product content and the customer journey across desktop and mobile.

The gallery contains original captures of the working Karji Perfumes website taken for this portfolio. They show the public storefront and its product-discovery content. The case study describes the supplied project scope and visible experience; it does not attribute sales or conversion results to the build. Visit Karji Perfumes.

/ The customer journey

How the workflow works

The Karji Perfumes journey begins with fragrance and brand navigation and moves towards a relevant product or enquiry. Watches and accessories and product comparison and discovery help explain the range. Clear product information supports the next action without making visitors rely only on imagery.

  • Fragrance and brand navigation
  • Watches and accessories
  • Product comparison and discovery
  • Collection and product content

/ Design and development focus

Design and development focus

The work connects collection and product content with reusable storefront patterns. Category labels, product information and mobile interactions should remain consistent across the catalogue. The case study focuses on the customer-facing work rather than claiming unverified backend or fulfilment integrations.

  • Watches and accessories
  • Product comparison and discovery
  • Collection and product content
  • Mobile shopping journeys
  • Storefront content structure
