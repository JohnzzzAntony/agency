Dark or Light Mode: Choosing Readable Website UI

DN DuooNex editorial · September 21, 2026 DuooNex editorial · September 21, 2026
UI UX

Dark and light interfaces can both work well. The better choice depends on the content, viewing conditions and tasks your audience needs to complete, rather than a blanket rule about aesthetics.

Prioritize readability and content

Check body copy, form labels, focus indicators and important actions in the chosen palette. Product photography and dense tables may need different treatments from a promotional hero.

Practical review checklist

  • Review text contrast across default, hover and disabled states.
  • Check product images against each background treatment.
  • Keep validation messages and keyboard focus easy to distinguish.
  • Test on actual mobile screens in different lighting conditions.

Document the design rules

Use semantic color roles in the design system so future screens remain consistent. DuooNex can translate approved Figma styles into responsive components and review them in the working website.
